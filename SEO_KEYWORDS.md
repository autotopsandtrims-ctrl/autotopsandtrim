## The honest caveat

National informational traffic is **not** Monroe customers. Someone in Ohio
reading "how to fix a sagging headliner" will never drive to NC. The value of
section 3 is (a) building any domain authority at all from a standing start of
zero, (b) internal links into the six service pages, (c) the slice of searchers
who are actually local. It is **not** a direct lead engine — the GBP and the
local pack are.

Ranked by actual expected leads:
1. **Recover the Google Business Profile.** Nothing else is close.
2. Fix the apex cert and the Formspree endpoint (leads currently flow to an
   unverified destination).
3. The cost cluster (3a/3b) — buyer intent, KD 0.
4. `marine upholstery` + boat repair (3d) — a real differentiator, almost no
   competition.
5. The DIY headliner cluster (3c) — biggest volume, weakest intent.
