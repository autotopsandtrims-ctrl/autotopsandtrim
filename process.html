How it works

What happens after you call

No mystery and no deposit to find out what a job costs. Here is the whole sequence, start to finish.

Call (980) 385-8101 Request a quote
01Step by step

Five steps, and you are told the price before step three

01
Walk in or call ahead

You bring it in

Drive the vehicle over, trailer the boat, or carry in a single seat. We look at it with you and talk through what you want.

02
Free, and itemised

We quote in person

We check what is under the cover — foam, frames, pads, bows — because that is where surprises live. Then you get an itemised estimate at no charge.

03
Samples in hand

You pick the materials

We keep samples in the shop. Vinyl or canvas, glass or plastic window, period-correct or upgraded — you see and feel the difference before deciding.

04
Same hands throughout

We do the work

Disassembly, repair of what is underneath, then cutting and stitching. The same hands that quoted the job do the work.

05
Checked with you

Fitting and finish

Nothing leaves until it fits properly. Weather sealing on marine and convertible work, and a final check with you at pickup.

02The estimate

What the free estimate actually covers

An estimate that only prices the fabric is not an estimate. Here is everything we look at before we give you a number.

01

The cover itself

Material, colour and stitch — the part every quote includes.

02

What is underneath

Foam, frames, pads and bows. This is the part most quotes leave out.

03

The windows and seals

Rear windows, weather sealing and anything that will let water in.

04

The realistic timing

How long it will actually take, told to you before you commit.
